LLM Council briefing

E6 contact-reasoning redesign

Five independent perspectives, anonymous peer review, and chairman synthesis.

The question

Should GSET E6 remove its two-stage VLM method—a hypothetical full-contact baseline inferred from observations that may not be full-contact, followed by a transferred contact delta—and what is a scientifically better way to reason about projected contact fractions?

Council verdict

Retire the current two-stage E6 as a quantitative method. Replace it first with a pointwise, one-stage E6-joint condition that exposes complete metadata, keeps controlled pairs local, labels interpolation versus extrapolation, and preserves raw responses.

Do not silently enforce monotonicity. The proxy-to-force relationship must be tested with replicated controlled sweeps before it becomes a model constraint. Batch prediction and deterministic correction should remain separately reported ablations.

Open tension

VLM numerical estimator vs evidence structurer; pointwise vs batched; diagnose vs enforce monotonicity.

Where the council agrees

The current “full-contact baseline” is not identifiable because retrieved anchors may not represent full contact. Adding a transferred delta can double-count contact. The creatine pair establishes one local finite difference, not a universal response law. Every observation should remain a complete tuple of force, mass, roughness, projected-geometry proxy, and material/geometry evidence.

Where the council clashes

  • Whether same-object contact sweeps should be predicted pointwise or jointly.
  • Whether monotonicity should only be measured or eventually enforced.
  • Whether the VLM should produce the numerical force or structure evidence for a regression, Gaussian process, or hierarchical model.

Blind spots caught in peer review

  • projected_contact_fraction may not measure real engaged adhesive contact; pressure distribution, peel, deformation, and failure mode can change with it.
  • Monotonicity is a hypothesis, not yet an established law.
  • Replicated force trials, proxy uncertainty, leakage-safe surface splits, and repeated VLM runs are required.
  • VLM-generated ranges are not calibrated uncertainty intervals.
  • Batching counterfactual values gives the model extra relational information and must be evaluated as a separate task.

Recommended replacement

  1. Reveal the actual proxy value for every retrieved observation.
  2. Separate general analogues from same-surface controlled variants.
  3. Describe controlled effects as local evidence, never a transferable coefficient.
  4. Ask for a direct point estimate, evidence-strength label, and interpolation/extrapolation status.
  5. Compare E5, legacy E6, one-stage E6-joint, and VLM-structured-evidence plus a conventional numerical model.
  6. Report raw outputs, MAE, repeat variance, and monotonicity diagnostics; add calibrated intervals only with a calibration method.

First action: freeze legacy E6 and implement the complete-metadata, pointwise E6-joint prompt.

Advisor responses

The Contrarian

Remove the method. The full-contact baseline is undefined from the supplied evidence, while hidden contact remains latent in measured force. One controlled pair identifies one finite difference for one interface, not a transferable curve. Use complete references, ranges, and explicit underidentification.

The First Principles Thinker

Every observation must remain a joint tuple. Controlled variants provide local direction and sensitivity only. Use direct evidence-conditioned prediction, mark interpolation versus extrapolation, and never claim a universal additive penalty.

The Expansionist

A one-stage E6 can become a clean ablation of whether transparent contact evidence improves held-out prediction. Later, multiple controlled sweeps can support surface-specific or hierarchical response shapes.

The Outsider

The terminology itself overclaims. Consider renaming the feature to projected geometry ratio until it is validated as engaged contact. Separate image-force prediction from learning a contact-response law.

The Executor

Implement pointwise E6-joint quickly, preserve raw outputs, and compare it with E5 and separately declared joint/monotonic variants. Score error, repeat variance, interval calibration, and consistency separately.

Peer-review highlights

Reviewers favored the Executor for falsifiability and the Outsider for construct validity. Their strongest shared warning was that monotonicity itself remains unvalidated. They also identified measurement noise, split leakage, retrieval-policy confounding, and uncalibrated VLM confidence as unresolved threats.
